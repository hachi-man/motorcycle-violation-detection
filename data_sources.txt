Dataset Sources — Motorcycle Violation Detection System
======================================================

All datasets used are publicly available and used for educational and research purposes.

SOURCE 1 — Roboflow Universe: Helmet and No-Helmet Rider Detection
URL: https://universe.roboflow.com (search: "helmet no helmet rider detection")
License: Public (CC BY 4.0)
Usage: Helmet and no_helmet class images
Approximate images used: varies by dataset version

SOURCE 2 — Kaggle: HelmetViolations Dataset
URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pkdarabi/helmetviolations
License: Public
Usage: Supplementary helmet and no_helmet images
Images: 1,004 original images

SOURCE 3 — Roboflow Universe: Triple Ride Detection
URL: https://universe.roboflow.com (search: "triple ride detection")
License: Public
Usage: motorcycle_overloaded class images
Approximate images used: varies by dataset version

SOURCE 4 — YouTube: Pakistani Road Footage (self-extracted frames)
Sources: publicly available driving footage from Pakistani cities
Method: frames extracted using yt-dlp and ffmpeg at 1 frame per 4 seconds
Usage: authentic Pakistani road context, motorcycle and rider images
Credit: various YouTube content creators (public videos)

TOTAL DATASET AFTER CLEANING AND ANNOTATION
--------------------------------------------
Training images:   405
Validation images: 116
Test images:        57
Total:             578

Annotation tool: Roboflow Annotate
Export format: YOLOv8 (YOLO txt format)
Train/Val/Test split: 70% / 20% / 10%
